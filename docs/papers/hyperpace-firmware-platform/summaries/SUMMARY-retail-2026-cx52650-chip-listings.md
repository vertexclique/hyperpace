# SUMMARY: retail listings naming the CX52650 receiver chip

**Claim.** Multiple retail listings name the CX52650/CX52650P as the dongle MCU paired with a CX52850 mouse MCU (Endgame Gear XM2we) or with a Nordic 52840 mouse (MONKA M3, "Shanghai Botong (Jiangmeng) CX52650P"), and AJAZZ AJ139 uses a "CX52650P chip". Confirms CX52650 is a real, shipping Compx/Beken dongle part.

**Method.** One page fetched (MONKA M3); others are search snippets.

**Result.** Corroborates the chip family exists and is a dongle-side MCU.

**Evidence tier.** 4 (retail marketing copy).

**Performance.** Marketing claims sub-1ms internal latency; not independently checked.

**Correctness.** Proven only that the part number is used commercially. The exact "N" suffix (CX52650N) and its bootloader are not in any listing.

**Relevance to hyperpace.** Confirms the 1K/2K receiver chip class is real and Beken-derived, but gives no update mechanism. Reinforces that CX52650N flashing must be treated as Compx-proprietary and unverified.
